# Handoff Brief — Platforms and Authorities

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19
**Use:** prioritisation and evidence handling. This is not a target list.

## 1. What to act on

Prioritise **behaviour and harm**, not the UTTP label alone:

1. **Youth-targeted grooming/coercion** — especially where a minor is directed to
   self-harm, produce sexual material, or harm others.
2. **Sextortion/blackmail** — threats to release intimate or compromising material.
3. **Doxxing/swatting threats** — release of private data or false emergency reports.
4. **Coordinated mass reporting/mention spam** — abuse of platform reporting and
   notification systems.
5. **Shock-content recruitment** — gore/animal cruelty/self-harm content used as
   status or entry tests.
6. **Cross-platform migration** — the same behaviour reappearing under a new brand.

## 2. Platform actions

- Enforce on **conduct**, not only usernames containing “UTTP.”
- Preserve evidence at report time: URL, timestamp, context, and outcome.
- Look for **cross-account and cross-platform patterns**, not just individual posts.
- Prioritise youth-facing surfaces: comments, live chat, DMs, servers, gaming.
- Track 2026 labels as **watch indicators**: Circusfall, UTTP Forum,
  OfficerSlappy faction, AFR, Bn0r Empire, RDF/RDFPARTY, DPOS.
- Do not publish or circulate member lists; use group-level indicators.

## 3. Authority actions

- Treat exploitation/coercion as the primary offence, not “membership in UTTP.”
- Use the designated-entity framework where it applies: **764** (Canada) and
  **O9A/ONA** (New Zealand).
- Request platform preservation early; migration and deletion are fast.
- Do not rely on “FBI Tier 1” language; no primary source using that label was
  located in this review. Use the Canadian designation and FBI/DOJ cases.
- UTTP itself is **not** designated in the sources reviewed; do not assert that it is.

## 4. Research/community actions

- Preserve public evidence; do not infiltrate, bait, or engage.
- Label confidence: E/C/A/S/D/X.
- Separate **area** (platform/surface) from **account** (person).
- Do not convert a 200-row area register into a target list.
- Report imminent harm to the authorities.

## 5. Escalation triggers

Escalate immediately when there is:

- a credible threat to a minor or identifiable victim;
- sexual exploitation material or sextortion;
- a credible swatting/mass-casualty threat;
- a platform’s repeated non-response to documented exploitation.

## 6. What this brief does not do

- It does not identify individuals.
- It does not authorise contact, infiltration, or retaliation.
- It does not replace legal process or platform investigation.
- It does not claim UTTP is a designated entity.

## 7. One-page priority

> **Focus on exploitative conduct and youth targeting. Use UTTP/DPOS/764 as
> context, not as a substitute for evidence. Preserve early; escalate harm;
> do not publish people.**
