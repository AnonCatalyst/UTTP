# Metrics — Is Pressure Working?

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19

The goal is not “UTTP is dead.” The goal is **less reach, less recruitment access,
less deniability, and more lawful action**.

## 1. Outcome metrics

| Metric | What it measures | Preferred direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active public UTTP-branded spaces | Brand reach/platform persistence | Down |
| New-brand survival (3-month) | Whether splinters persist | Down |
| Youth-facing recruitment access | Access to minors/vulnerable users | Down |
| Enforcement outcomes | Removals, bans, arrests, designations, cases | Up |
| Platform response rate | Reports acted on with outcome | Up |
| Time-to-removal | Speed of platform action | Down |
| Deniability | “It was just trolling” claims surviving scrutiny | Down |

## 2. Evidence metrics

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| Claims with primary/independent source | Evidence quality |
| Claims downgraded/quarantined | Self-correction rate |
| Broken links replaced | Record durability |
| Offshoot claims verified vs `D/S` | Research maturity |
| Area rows with observed/reported status | Coverage quality |

## 3. Watch metrics (90 days)

- Does AFR persist after the antifandom.org shutdown? **D/S**
- Do RDF/RDFPARTY and Bn0r Empire survive as distinct brands? **D/S**
- Does Rockstarfall continue past October 2026? **D/S**
- Does DPOS retain a structured server or fragment further? **C/D**
- Do platforms enforce on behaviour rather than the UTTP token? **Outcome**
- Do authorities cite 764/O9A designations without mislabeling UTTP? **Outcome**

## 4. Anti-metrics

Do **not** use:

- number of handles listed;
- number of names in a document;
- number of area rows as a proxy for members;
- “complete DNB” as a measured census;
- engagement/retaliation volume as success.

## 5. Review cadence

- **Monthly:** new offshoot labels, platform removals, enforcement news.
- **Quarterly:** current-state brief refresh; watchlist status.
- **On event:** credible violence/exploitation threat; designation; arrest;
  major platform action.

## 6. Success statement

> Success is not that UTTP disappears from the internet. Success is that its
> factions have less access to young people, less ability to deny what they do,
> and more lawful consequences when they cross a line.
