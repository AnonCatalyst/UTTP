# 764 → O9A — Sourced Trace

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19
**Question:** what connects the 764 network to the Order of Nine Angles (O9A)?
**Answer in one sentence:** New Zealand's O9A statement of case lists 764 as an
O9A **nexion**, and independent research documents O9A influence on 764; this is a
network/lineage relationship inside a decentralised ecosystem, **not** a command
chain or corporate parent relationship.

---

## 1. The primary finding

New Zealand's **Statement of Case to Designate the Order of the Nine Angles as a
Terrorist Entity** (December 2025) states:

- O9A is a transnational, informally connected network of cells. **E**
- O9A is decentralised and uses leaderless resistance; acolytes are clustered in
  autonomous cells called **nexions**, which are **operationally independent**. **E**
- O9A has **no established leaders that directly conduct terrorism**; it relies on
  infiltration and inspiration. **E**
- Known violent-extremist nexions include **The Tempel Ov Blood, The Satanic
  Front, The Black Order of Vinland, The Mortis Covenant, 764, No Lives Matter,
  and Maniac Murder Cult**. **E**
- 764 is described as a transnational violent online network that targets and
  exploits children and vulnerable people. **E**
- MKY recruits from 764 and NLM and is linked to O9A aesthetics and publications. **E**

This is the strongest public evidence for the 764–O9A link. It is a **government
statement of case**, not a court conviction, but it is a primary legal instrument
prepared for designation.

> The statement is at `statement-of-case-order-of-nine-angles-terrorist-designation.pdf`
> on the New Zealand Police publication page. The Gazette notice is **2025-au7135**.

## 2. What "nexion" means

A **nexion** is O9A's term for an autonomous cell. The statement of case says:

- nexions align with O9A's Satanism and militant accelerationism;
- they are **operationally independent**;
- they act clandestinely and avoid attention.

So "764 is an O9A nexion" means:

- 764 is **inside the O9A ecosystem**;
- 764 is **not** a subsidiary with a reporting line;
- O9A does **not** have a central commander issuing orders to 764.

This is why the archive should not use "parent company," "operational arm," or
"O9A commands 764" without qualification.

## 5. Pathways of influence

The record supports several mechanisms. They are not mutually exclusive.

### 5.1 Textual and publishing influence

O9A's corpus is fluid, adaptable, and widely circulated. The US-based nexion
Tempel Ov Blood and its Martinet Press are described in the NZ statement of case
as prolific disseminators of violent extremist content, including neo-Nazi
material and exploitation. **E**

That publishing layer is a transmission path: texts and aesthetics reach groups
that are not formal O9A nexions.

### 5.2 Aesthetic and symbolic adoption

764 material uses O9A-linked Satanic/occult and accelerationist imagery. Reporting
and research describe this as inspiration or adoption, not necessarily doctrinal
membership. **C**

### 5.3 Status-through-transgression

O9A texts frame transgression and violence as spiritually or status-signalling
acts. 764's status economy rewards cruelty and abuse. The overlap is in the
**logic of escalation**, not necessarily in a shared chain of command. **A/C**

### 5.4 Online co-location

764, NLM, MKY, and O9A-adjacent actors circulate in overlapping Telegram,
Discord, and Com/Terrorgram spaces. Researchers document crossover in membership
and material. **C**

### 5.5 Self-radicalisation

O9A is designed for self-initiation and leaderless resistance. That makes direct
recruitment unnecessary: an individual can adopt the corpus without joining a
nexion. This complicates claims of organisational membership. **E/A**

## 6. What this trace proves — and does not prove

### Proven / established

1. New Zealand designated O9A on 2025-12-07. **E**
2. Canada designated 764 on 2025-12-10. **E**
3. The NZ O9A statement of case lists 764 as an O9A nexion. **E**
4. O9A nexions are autonomous and operationally independent. **E**
5. O9A has no established central leaders directing terrorism. **E**
6. Independent research documents O9A influence on 764. **C**

### Assessed

1. 764 functions as an O9A-linked violent network with substantial autonomy. **A**
2. O9A provides a legitimising vocabulary and aesthetic, not a command staff. **A**
3. Some 764 members may also be O9A adherents; membership is fluid. **A/C**

### Not proven

1. O9A centrally commands 764. **No evidence located.**
2. 764 is identical to O9A. **No.**
3. O9A created 764. **No evidence located.**
4. Every 764 action is directed by O9A. **No.**
5. UTTP is O9A or is designated because O9A is designated. **No.**

## 7. Corrections to earlier archive language

Some earlier WIC documents used this ladder:

> O9A (parent ideology) → 764 (operational arm) → UTTP (recruitment pipeline)

That formulation is useful as a **heuristic**, but it overstates command and
hierarchy. Corrected language:

> O9A is a decentralised ideological ecosystem. 764 is a designated network
> documented as one of its nexions. UTTP is an NVE-adjacent harassment brand with
> assessed overlap to 764. These are network positions, not a corporate chain.

The folder name `05_PARENT_ENTITIES/09A Cult/` is retained for provenance. It is
an archive-organisation label, not a proven command relationship.
