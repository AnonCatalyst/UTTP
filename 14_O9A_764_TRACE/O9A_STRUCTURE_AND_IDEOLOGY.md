# O9A — Structure, Ideology, and Recruitment Logic

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19
**Purpose:** explain how O9A is organised and why it spreads, without reproducing
operational material.

---

## 1. What kind of network is O9A?

O9A is best understood as three things at once:

1. **A corpus** — texts, symbols, and a "Sinister Tradition" that anyone can read.
2. **A network of nexions** — autonomous cells that self-identify with the corpus.
3. **An influence** — aesthetic and ideological material adopted by other groups,
   including 764, NLM, MKY, Atomwaffen Division, and parts of Terrorgram.

It is **not** a conventional organisation with a headquarters, membership card,
or central command. The New Zealand statement of case describes it as a
transnational, informally connected network of cells. **E**

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |

| Operational control | Independent; no central command |
| Visibility | Clandestine; avoids attention |
| Function | Recruitment, text dissemination, inspiration, and sometimes violence |

A nexion can be violent without every nexion being violent. The statement says
"not all nexions of O9A are inherently or fundamentally violent." **E**

## 3. Acolytes and adherents

O9A membership is not strictly hierarchical. The statement of case separates:

- **Acolytes** — active participants who create or spread texts, recruit, or
  manage nexions.
- **Adherents / "Dreccs"** — peripheral participants who may desire belonging but
  are not core members; described as the "foot soldiers" most likely to commit
  violence.

The relationship is described as **stochastic**: acolytes provide inspiration,
advice, or arms-length direction. This is a diffuse influence model, not a
reporting chain. **E**

## 4. Core ideological elements

### 4.1 Militant accelerationism

O9A seeks to accelerate the collapse of liberal democracy and the current order.
Violence is framed as a catalyst. The desired end state is an authoritarian,
racially ordered "Imperium." **E/C**

### 4.2 Esoteric Nazism

O9A fuses Satanic and occult themes with neo-Nazi politics. The symbolism is not
decorative: it provides a ritual language for radicalisation and a bridge to
other extremist scenes. **E/C**

### 4.3 The "Sinister Tradition"

The corpus presents transgression against social norms as a spiritual path. The
statement of case describes texts that encourage desensitisation to extreme
violence and abuse, "culling," and the breakdown of the current system. **E**

This profile does not quote or reproduce the operational content.

### 4.4 Insight roles

O9A encourages adherents to infiltrate institutions or extremist groups for
extended periods to embed ideas, recruit, or incite violence. The NZ statement of
case treats this as a core tactic. **E**

### 4.5 Leaderless resistance

O9A is designed to survive leadership losses. Individuals can self-initiate from
texts. This is why designating O9A is legally and operationally different from
designating a hierarchical group. **E/A**

## 5. Recruitment and radicalisation logic

The record supports a **pipeline-like** pattern without a single recruiter:

1. **Exposure** — O9A texts, symbols, and aesthetics circulate online.
2. **Identification** — a user adopts the vocabulary as a way to interpret
   grievance, alienation, or a desire for status.
3. **Escalation** — transgression and violence become proof of commitment.
4. **Affiliation** — the user may join or form a nexion, or may remain a
   self-identified adherent.
5. **Action** — in some cases, violence or criminality follows.

This is an **open-source radicalisation model**, not a centrally managed pipeline.
That distinction is critical when mapping 764 and UTTP. **A/C**

---

## 6. Why O9A is attractive to other networks

- **Low barrier to entry** — no central vetting; texts are available.
- **Adaptable canon** — groups can take what they need.
- **Status rewards** — transgression signals commitment.
- **Aesthetic toolkit** — symbols, names, and rituals are easy to copy.
- **Leaderless design** — disruption of one cell does not end the network.
- **Cross-platform persistence** — material migrates as platforms remove content.

These features explain why O9A influence appears in multiple networks without
requiring formal membership. **A/C**

---

## 8. Analytic cautions

- Do not treat "O9A" as a single actor with a single intent.
- Do not treat a nexion as a franchise under central command.
- Do not infer membership from aesthetics alone.
- Do not infer a command link from shared texts.
- Do not repeat graphic details or operational instructions.
- Do not add private identifiers or victim information.

---

## 9. Bottom line

O9A is a **decentralised, text-driven, leaderless accelerationist network** whose
nexions are operationally independent. It is powerful precisely because it does
not need a headquarters. 764 is documented as one of its nexions, but that means
**network position**, not corporate ownership or command.
