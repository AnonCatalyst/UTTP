## 5. Source limitations

- The NZ statement of case is a **statement of case**, not a court judgment.
- The Canadian listing is a legal instrument, not a trial record.
- Research on 764 is shaped by platform removals, victim privacy, and the
  difficulty of verifying fluid online membership.
- "Nexion" is an O9A term; do not translate it as "subsidiary" or "franchise."
- Do not cite social-media profiles, Telegram links, or community directories as
  proof of membership.
