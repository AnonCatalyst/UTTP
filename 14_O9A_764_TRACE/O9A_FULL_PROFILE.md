## 2. Origins

- O9A's philosophy and practice emerged in **Shropshire, England, in the 1970s**
  from writings published under the pseudonym **"Anton Long."** The writings are
  widely attributed to British neo-Nazi activist **David Myatt**, who has denied
  the identification. **C**
- The corpus was produced from the 1970s to about 2012 and became the canon of
  an esoteric Satanist current called the **"Sinister Tradition."** **C**
- O9A spread through books, magazines, and later the internet. It associated with
  and infiltrated neo-Nazi groups in the 1990s. Online proliferation after the
  late 2000s allowed its aesthetics and texts to spread into other extremist
  spaces. **C**

Independent researchers describe O9A as influential in the niche of occult,
esoteric, and Satanic neo-Nazi groups, and as a reference point in several
terrorism cases. **C**

## 3. Structure

O9A is **not** a conventional membership organisation with a headquarters and
chain of command.

| Element | Description | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nexions | Autonomous, clandestine cells; operationally independent | E |
| Acolytes | Active participants who produce/disseminate texts, recruit, or manage nexions | E |
| Adherents / "Dreccs" | Peripheral participants; often the foot soldiers of violence | E |
| Self-initiation | Membership is not based on central vetting; individuals self-initiate through texts and acts | E |
| Inner circle | Some texts claim one exists; little is known and it is not verified | C/A |
| Leaders | No established leaders directly conducting terrorism; influence is diffuse | E |

The New Zealand statement of case describes the acolyte–adherent relationship as
usually **stochastic**, with acolytes providing inspiration, advice, or
"arms-length" direction. That is a network of influence, not a command staff. **E**

## 4. Ideology

O9A combines:

- **militant accelerationism** — pressure social divisions to collapse liberal
  democracy and bring about a white ethnostate;
- **esoteric / occult National Socialism** — Satanic and occult themes fused with
  neo-Nazi politics;
- **the "Sinister Tradition"** — a body of texts and practices that treats
  transgression, violence, and social breakdown as spiritually meaningful;
- **"Aeonics"** — a cyclical view of civilisation, with the current order to be
  replaced by an "Imperium" and eventually an Aryan space-faring age;
- **insight roles** — infiltration of institutions or extremist groups to
  radicalise, recruit, or incite violence.

The statement of case describes O9A's aim as encouraging illegal "sinister acts,"
including extreme violence and sexual abuse, to accelerate the breakdown of the
current system. **E**

This profile deliberately does not reproduce operational instructions, graphic
abuse descriptions, or recruitment material. The relevant finding is the
**ideological architecture**, not the content.

## 5. Online ecosystem

O9A material circulates across mainstream and fringe platforms, including
Telegram, X, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. **E**

Its texts are deliberately fluid and adaptable. That makes them attractive to
people outside O9A who adopt only parts of the aesthetic or rhetoric. Researchers
therefore distinguish:

- **O9A as a network** — nexions and self-identified adherents;
- **O9A as a corpus** — texts and symbols used by other groups;
- **O9A as an influence** — individuals or groups who borrow the aesthetic
  without belonging to a nexion.

This distinction matters for 764 and UTTP analysis.

## 6. Relationship to 764

The New Zealand statement of case lists **764** among known O9A **nexions**,
alongside No Lives Matter (NLM) and Maniac Murder Cult (MKY). **E**

Independent research also records O9A influence on 764 through court records,
shared aesthetics, and the broader Com/Terrorgram ecosystem. **C**

But the same statement of case says nexions are **operationally independent** and
that O9A has **no established leaders directly conducting terrorism**. **E**

The correct formulation is therefore:

> 764 is documented inside O9A's nexion ecosystem and draws on O9A material; it is
> not the same organisation as O9A, and O9A does not command 764.

See [`764_TO_O9A_TRACE.md`](764_TO_O9A_TRACE.md) for the evidence chain.

## 7. Relationship to UTTP

The UTTP archive contains WIC and community claims that UTTP overlaps with the
764/O9A ecosystem. Those claims are **A/S** unless tied to a primary source.

What is better supported:

- UTTP is a decentralised harassment/NVE-adjacent brand. **C**
- UTTP and 764 overlap in recruitment logic, youth-heavy target ecology,
  status-through-harm, and cross-platform movement. **C**
- A UTTP splinter has been described in community material as O9A-style; that is
  a **D/S** lead, not proof of O9A membership. **D/S**

What is not supported:

- "UTTP is O9A."
- "UTTP is a designated terrorist entity."
- "O9A created or commands UTTP."
- "09A Cult is the parent company of UTTP."

## 8. Designations and legal status

- **New Zealand:** designated O9A/ONA as a terrorist entity on **2025-12-07**;
  Gazette notice **2025-au7135**, published 2025-12-10; corrigendum **2025-au7194**,
  11 December 2025. **E**
- **Canada:** designated **764** on 2025-12-10 (SOR/2025-263). Canada did not
  designate O9A in that instrument. **E**
- **United Kingdom / United States:** this review did not locate a designation of
  O9A itself. The UK has proscribed several O9A-influenced groups; the US has
  prosecuted O9A-linked individuals. Do not describe either as having designated
  O9A without a current primary source. **C/A**

## 9. Common misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| "O9A is one organisation with a leader." | It is a decentralised network of autonomous nexions. |
| "764 is O9A." | 764 is a listed O9A nexion but a distinct network with its own origins and operations. |
| "A nexion is a franchise under central command." | A nexion is an autonomous cell; the relationship is ideological and networked, not command-and-control. |
| "09A Cult is O9A's headquarters." | "09A Cult" is an archive/community label; no such headquarters is established. |
| "O9A is only an online aesthetic." | It has a documented corpus, real-world cases, and a NZ designation; the aesthetic is one layer, not the whole. |
| "Designating O9A designates UTTP." | No. Designations are entity-specific. |

## 10. Why this matters

O9A is important to the UTTP/764 archive because it supplies:

- a **legitimising vocabulary** for violence and transgression;
- a **cell model** that survives leadership losses;
- a **publishing ecosystem** that spreads texts across groups;
- an **online recruitment grammar** that other networks can borrow.

It does **not** supply a single command structure. Analysts should describe
influence and network position, not a corporate hierarchy.
