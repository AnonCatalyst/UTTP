## 5. Corrections to earlier archive language

1. **Old notice number.** Earlier drafts cited NZ Gazette `2025-go11755`. The O9A
   designation is **2025-au7135** (published 2025-12-10), with corrigendum
   **2025-au7194** (2025-12-11). **X** for the old number.
2. **"Parent entity."** The folder `05_PARENT_ENTITIES/09A Cult/` uses "parent" as
   an archive-organisation label. It is not proof of command. **A** for context;
   **X** for "parent company."
3. **"Operational arm."** Calling 764 O9A's "operational arm" overstates
   coordination. Better: "a designated network documented as an O9A nexion." **A**
4. **"Hierarchy confirmed."** The NZ statement establishes a nexion listing and a
   decentralised structure, not a command hierarchy. **A/X**
5. **"UTTP–764–O9A pipeline."** The pipeline is a useful assessment model for
   radicalisation and overlap, not a proven command chain. **A**

## 6. What would strengthen the record

- Platform transparency data on cross-membership.
- Primary Canadian listing documents describing 764's ideology.
- Primary NZ statement of case (already available) and any subsequent legal
  challenges or corrigenda.
- Independent research using primary chat data with ethical and legal safeguards.

New evidence should be added without private identifiers, victim data, or
operational instructions.

## 7. Ethical and legal limits

- Do not contact, infiltrate, bait, or provoke any group or person.
- Do not publish private identifiers, addresses, or victim information.
- Do not reproduce graphic abuse material or operational manuals.
- Do not turn analysis into a target list.
- Report imminent harm or exploitation to the appropriate authorities:
  [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://www.cybertip.org), [FBI IC3](https://www.ic3.gov).

## 8. Bottom line

The evidence supports a **documented network/lineage relationship**:

> 764 is a designated network listed as an O9A nexion in New Zealand's statement
> of case, within a decentralised ecosystem whose cells are operationally
> independent.

It does **not** support identity, central command, or a corporate parent
relationship. The precise sentence matters.
