## 3. What the designations do — and do not do

### They do

- Establish a legal status for the named entity in that jurisdiction.
- Trigger property, finance, and material-support offences.
- Provide a government finding that can be cited as **E**.
- Signal that the entity meets the statutory terrorist-entity criteria.

### They do not

- Designate every affiliated or overlapping group.
- Convert an allegation about UTTP into a designation.
- Establish a command relationship between O9A and 764.
- Make "O9A" a single hierarchical organisation.
- Automatically apply in other countries.
