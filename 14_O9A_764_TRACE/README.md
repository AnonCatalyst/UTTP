## Why this folder exists

Earlier archive material described **764 → O9A** as a hierarchy and placed
"09A Cult" under `05_PARENT_ENTITIES/`. That language was too strong. The New
Zealand statement of case does describe 764 as an O9A **nexion**, but O9A is a
decentralised, leaderless network and a nexion is an autonomous cell — not a
corporate subsidiary or a command relationship.

This folder does three things:

1. Traces the documented 764–O9A relationship from primary and independent sources.
2. Profiles O9A as a decentralised militant-accelerationist network.
3. States the limits of the evidence, including claims the archive should stop repeating.

## Bottom line

- **New Zealand designated O9A** as a terrorist entity on **2025-12-07**; the
  Gazette notice is **2025-au7135**, published 2025-12-10. **E**
- **Canada designated 764** on **2025-12-10**; the regulation is **SOR/2025-263**. **E**
- New Zealand's O9A statement of case lists **764, No Lives Matter, and Maniac
  Murder Cult** among O9A **nexions**. **E** (primary statement of case)
- O9A is **decentralised and leaderless**; nexions are **operationally
  independent**. **E**
- Therefore the correct finding is: **764 is documented inside O9A's nexion
  ecosystem**, not "764 is O9A" and not "O9A commands 764." **C/A**
- **UTTP is not a designated terrorist entity** in the sources reviewed. Its
  link to this ecosystem is an assessed/overlap claim, not a designation. **X** for a UTTP designation; **A/C** for overlap.
