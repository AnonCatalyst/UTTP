## What the timeline does not establish

- It does not establish that O9A centrally commands 764.

- It does not establish that every O9A nexion is violent.
- It does not convert a statement of case into a court conviction.

## Date note

The New Zealand **designation** occurred on **7 December 2025**. The Gazette
**notice** is dated **10 December 2025**. Both dates are correct for different
events; do not collapse them without saying which one you mean.
