## 1. What DPOS is

**DPOS** stands for **Degeneracy Police Order State**. Archive captures also show
the shorthand **“Degeneracy Police OS”** and “Degen Police.”

It is best understood as:

- a **Discord-based, heavily role-played “anti-degeneracy” network**;
- a **hardcore UTTP offshoot/splinter** that inherited UTTP’s raid culture and
  extreme content;
- a **structured harassment brand** with ranks, departments, “task force” and
  “intelligence” channels, and a police-state aesthetic;
- a **bridge node** between UTTP-style trolling and 764/Com-style exploitation
  and grooming.

It is **not**:

- a real government, police force, or state;
- a designated terrorist entity in the sources reviewed;
- proven to be a 764 unit, a 764 command, or a merged 764 organisation.

**Acronym warning:** outside this archive, “DPOS” commonly means “Delegated Proof
of Stake” in blockchain contexts. This dossier uses DPOS only for *Degeneracy
Police Order State*.

## 2. Why DPOS matters to the UTTP investigation

DPOS is the clearest example of **UTTP’s next stage**:

1. **It turns a loose troll brand into a structured operation.** UTTP is a
   decentralised brand; DPOS mimics a state with departments, ranks, logs,
   tribunals, and “intelligence” channels.
2. **It gives UTTP plausible deniability.** UTTP can distance itself from DPOS
   while sharing members, targets, and tactics.
3. **It is a recruitment escalator.** UTTP-style raids and fandom drama provide
   the entry point; DPOS provides the “hardcore” identity and escalation.
4. **It is a bridge to 764/Com.** DPOS combines anti-degeneracy moralism with
   grooming, blackmail, self-harm coercion, and shock content associated with
   764/Com.
5. **It targets the same youth-heavy spaces.** FPE, Gacha, Roblox, anime, and
   fandom communities are the shared hunting ground.
6. **It is a succession node.** When UTTP fractures, DPOS-style branding shows
   how the network can re-form under a more extreme name.

That is why DPOS is not just another splinter: it is the **organisational and
ideological hinge** between UTTP’s harassment ecosystem and the 764/Com
exploitation ecosystem.

- “National Capitalism,” “Empire,” “state,” and police/iron-fist slogans;

**Assessment:** the political content functions primarily as **camouflage,
recruitment bait, and status language**. It gives the group a moral vocabulary
for harassment and a ready-made enemy list. The archive does not establish a
coherent political programme or a formal party.

### 7.1 UTTP

DPOS is the **hardcore successor brand**: it inherits UTTP’s enemies, raid
tactics, and youth-facing target ecology, then adds a state-like structure and a
more explicit extremist aesthetic. The link is strong at the level of lineage and
personnel, weaker at the level of current command.

### 7.2 764

The DPOS–764 relationship is **overlap, not identity**. The overlap includes
grooming/blackmail/self-harm patterns and shock content. No evidence establishes
that 764 commands DPOS, that DPOS is a 764 unit, or that the two merged. Claims of
“764-derived 100%” are overclaims.

### 7.3 O9A

O9A appears as an **aesthetic/ideological influence** in the broader ecosystem.
There is no evidence that O9A directs DPOS. Do not flatten O9A, 764, and DPOS into
one organisation.

### 7.4 Spawnism

Community and leak material allege DPOS crossover with Spawnism self-harm
recruitment. This is **D/S** — a serious lead, not an established command link.

### 7.5 FPE

FPE is a **target ecosystem**, not an ally. DPOS-style actors are documented in
FPE harassment/doxxing allegations. WIC’s FPE record is self-authored and should
be read as **A**, not independent proof.

### 7.6 Adjacent UTTP ecosystem

DPOS is linked by individual crossover to TNM/148/GRS/Enclave and other raid
crews. These are **personnel/ecology links**, not proof of a unified front.

### 7.7 Defenders

GateKeeper and community defenders track/blacklist DPOS actors. That is a
**defence relationship**, not an affiliation. A third-party OSINT group is a research source, not a
partner or member.
