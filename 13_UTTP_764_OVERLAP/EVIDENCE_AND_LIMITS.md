# Evidence and Limits

**Question:** what does it mean to “prove” a UTTP–764 overlap?

## 1. Evidence hierarchy

| Level | Example | What it can prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E — Established | Canada’s 764 designation; court records; direct public observation | 764 is a designated NVE network; specific conduct in a case |
| C — Corroborated | Two independent sources; independent analysis + primary source | A pattern or overlap exists beyond one source |
| A — Assessment | WIC analysis of the archive | A reasoned inference from the record |
| S — Single-source | One interview/comment/leak | A lead, not a finding |
| D — Community | Wiki, directory, donated note | Context/lead; not independent verification |
| X — Disputed | Old “10 GB” claim; “FBI Tier 1”; “UTTP is designated” | Do not repeat as fact |

## 2. What is proven

1. **764 is designated in Canada** and is a serious NVE/child-exploitation
   network. **E**
2. **764 and O9A overlap** in the same extremist ecosystem; NZ designated O9A. **E/C**
3. **UTTP has a long history of harassment, raids, doxxing threats, mass
   reporting, and platform migration.** **C**
4. **UTTP and 764 overlap in tactic families and membership fluidity**, according
   to independent analysis (Sparx) and the broader research record. **C**
5. **UTTP targets youth-heavy fandom/gaming spaces**, and 764 targets youth in
   gaming/Discord/Telegram spaces. **C**
6. **Both use status-through-harm and cross-platform movement.** **C**

## 3. What is assessed, not proven

1. A deliberate UTTP leadership decision to recruit 764/Foodist members. **A/S**
2. A direct UTTP→764 command or coordination relationship. **A/S**
3. UTTP-wide sextortion/CSAM conduct. **A/S** — 764 is established; UTTP-specific
   cases require primary evidence.
4. Shared manuals/training. **A/D**
5. Offline violence direction from UTTP to 764. **A/D**

## 4. What is not supported

- “UTTP is 764.”
- “UTTP is a designated terrorist entity.”
- “764 is a parent company of UTTP.”
- “Every UTTP faction is 764.”
- “The FPE operation is independent proof of a 764 command link.”

## 5. What would strengthen the case

- Primary chat/forum records with provenance.
- Court documents naming UTTP-linked defendants and 764 coordination.
- Independent platform trust-and-safety data.
- A second independent research institution repeating the overlap finding.
- A government instrument naming UTTP itself (if one exists).

## 6. Ethical limits

- Do not infiltrate, join, bait, or provoke.
- Do not publish private identifiers or victim data.
- Do not reproduce graphic content.
- Do not turn analysis into a target list.
- Report imminent harm/exploitation to the authorities.

## 7. Bottom line

The evidence supports **overlap in tactics and membership ecology**. It does not
support **identity, command, or designation**. The correct sentence is:

> “UTTP is an NVE-adjacent network that overlaps with 764 in recruitment logic,
> target ecology, status incentives, and cross-platform movement; 764 is the
> designated entity, not UTTP.”
