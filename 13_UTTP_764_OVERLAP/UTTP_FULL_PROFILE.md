# UTTP — Full Group Profile

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-19
**Confidence labels:** E/C/A/S/D/X — see `00_READ_FIRST/CLAIM_LEDGER.md`.

---

## 1. Identity

**UTTP** = UTube Troll Police / YouTube Troll Police. A decentralised online
harassment network and brand, founded in 2011, historically active on YouTube,
Google+, Discord, and X. The name functions as a **brand/overcoat**: a faction can
be removed while the name and playbook are reused.

**Not:** a single company, a single Discord server, a single leader, a designated
terrorist entity in the sources reviewed.

## 4. Platforms and targets

- **Platforms:** YouTube, Google+, Discord, X; community reports also place
  splinters on Telegram/TikTok and soycord/COM-adjacent spaces.
- **Targets:** fandoms, creators, wikis, youth-heavy communities; examples in the
  archive include FPE, TADC/Murder Drones, Gacha Life, Countryball, and other
  web-animation fandoms.
- **Why these targets:** large audiences, young users, high drama/engagement, and
  weak moderation/documentation.

## 6. Current state (late 2026)

Community documentation describes UTTP as “complete DNB”/almost no members after
the 2026 split and bans, while splinters and adjacent networks continue. This is
a **community assessment**, not a measured census. The brand remains reusable;
the faction ecosystem is fragmented.

**764 relationship (2026):** edge fusion/overlap, not merger. No evidence located
that UTTP is an operational arm of 764; see
[`FINAL_ASSESSMENT_2026-11-19.md`](FINAL_ASSESSMENT_2026-11-19.md).

---

## 7. Why it persists

1. Brand reuse after takedowns.
2. Decentralised factions and fluid membership.
3. Platform enforcement gaps and slow appeals.
4. Youth-heavy target spaces.
5. Cheap anonymity and cross-platform migration.
6. Documentation gaps that make harm unprovable.

---

## 8. What would change this profile

- Primary evidence of a current unified command.
- A government designation naming UTTP itself.
- Independent measurement of active UTTP-branded spaces.
- Court/primary records for specific recruitment claims.
- A verified end to the brand’s reuse (not just one server).
