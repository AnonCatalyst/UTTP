# Final Assessment — Is UTTP an Operational Arm of 764?

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19
**Question:** Is UTTP an arm of 764, was it infiltrated by 764, or is it an
operational arm of 764 today because it merged with 764?
**Short answer:** The evidence supports **edge fusion and membership overlap**.
It does **not** support a formal merger, an operational-arm relationship, or a
764-directed infiltration of UTTP.

## 2. What the strongest sources actually say

### 2.1 Sparx Intelligence (2026-07-15) — C

Sparx describes UTTP as:

- a nihilistic violent extremist network influenced by accelerationism;
- overlapping with **764, NLM, and Maniac Murder Cult**;
- decentralised, with multiple splinter groups and fluid membership;
- using grooming “similarly to 764”;
- a space where “764 and the UTTP interlope in terms of membership”;
- linked to a **Doxbin dox conducted by various members of the UTTP and 764**.

Sparx also says:

> “an infiltration of bad actors between 2019 and 2021 led to the spread of
> violent accelerationist ideologies closely associated with O9A.”

That is evidence of **infiltration by extremist actors** and **membership
overlap**. It is not evidence that 764, as an organisation, infiltrated UTTP.
ISD dates 764’s emergence to 2021, which makes a 2019–2021 “764 infiltration”
chronologically weak.

### 2.2 Bx Writes (2026-06-25) — C/A

Bx Writes, reporting on Com/764 cases, states:

> “The Foodists, along with related groups such as UTTP, have been around for many
> years and have only recently begun to fuse with parts of extortion Com, like the
> 764 network.”

The same article describes the Venn diagram between these groups “migrating to a
single circle” over the previous year.

This supports **fusion at the edges**. It does not describe a merger, a chain of
command, or UTTP becoming 764’s operational arm.

### 2.3 Community documentation — D/S

- **Soyjak Wiki** says UTTP became “intertwined with the 764 COM cult and its
  splinters around 2023–25” and helped harass, blackmail, or spread material.
  **D**
- A **Reddit r/truths** post claims UTTP “officially ‘partnered’ with 764.” No
  primary document, announcement, or independent confirmation was located.
  **D/S**
- These are leads/community claims, not proof of a merger or command.

### 2.4 DOJ complaint (2025) — E for the statement, not for an org link

A federal complaint quotes a defendant expressing support for both UTTP and 764
and referencing UTTP SWAT threats. That shows **individual-level co-ideation and
threat language**. It does not establish that UTTP and 764 are one organisation.

### 2.5 Government designations — E

- **Canada** designated **764** on 2025-12-10 (SOR/2025-263).
- **New Zealand** designated **O9A/ONA** on 2025-12-07 (Gazette 2025-au7135).
- No instrument located in this review designates **UTTP** or describes a
  UTTP–764 merger.

Designation of 764 does not designate its ecosystem, and it does not convert a
separate harassment brand into a 764 arm.

## 3. Why “operational arm” fails

“Operational arm” implies one or more of:

1. a command relationship;
2. tasking or coordination;
3. shared leadership or a merger;
4. a formal alliance with mutual obligations;
5. a public claim of merger by both parties.

The record supports **none of these at E/C level**.

- 764 is described by researchers and governments as **decentralised**, with
  cells and offshoots, not a central command.
- UTTP is also **decentralised**, with factions and splinters, not one command.
- Membership overlap and joint activity are documented, but overlap is not
  command.
- “Fusion” in the Bx/Sparx sense is **cultural, tactical, and membership-level**,
  not corporate or organisational.

A network can have shared members, shared targets, and joint actions without
being an “arm.” The correct concept is **coalescence** or **edge fusion**, not
merger.

---

## 4. Why “764 infiltrated UTTP” is not established

The claim has three problems:

1. **Chronology:** Sparx places the relevant infiltration in 2019–2021; 764
   emerged in 2021 according to ISD. A 764-specific infiltration of that period is not supported
   by the timeline.
2. **Specificity:** Sparx says “bad actors” and O9A-associated ideology, not “764
   operatives.”
3. **Direction:** Membership overlap can be individual movement in either
   direction; it does not prove a 764 infiltration campaign.

What is supported:

> UTTP was infiltrated by extremist/NVE-adjacent actors, some of whom moved in
> O9A/Com/764 spaces. That is not the same as a 764 operation.

## 5. What “today” looks like (late 2026)

- UTTP is **fragmented**: brand reuse, faction splits, an AFR splinter, and a
  community “complete DNB” assessment. **D**
- 764 remains a **designated, decentralised NVE network** with cells/offshoots. **E/C**
- Public reporting describes **fusion/overlap**, not a merger. **C/A**
- No evidence located of a unified UTTP–764 command, joint leadership, or formal
  merger in 2026. **X for merger/arm**
- UTTP remains **non-designated** in the instruments reviewed. **X for UTTP designation**

---

## 6. What would prove an operational-arm or merger claim

- A public merger announcement or organizational charter.
- Primary chat/forum records showing 764 tasking UTTP factions.
- Shared leadership roster with verifiable roles.
- Court document naming UTTP as a 764 component or co-conspirator entity.
- Government instrument describing the relationship.
- Platform trust-and-safety data showing coordinated command infrastructure.

Until then, the claim should be labelled **not established**, not asserted.

---

## 7. Bottom line

The strongest defensible update to the archive is:

> **UTTP is not a proven operational arm of 764 and is not shown to have merged
> with 764. The record supports a partially fused ecosystem: overlapping
> membership, shared tactics, joint harassment/doxing activity, and infiltration
> by NVE/O9A-adjacent actors. 764 is designated; UTTP is not.**

This preserves the serious overlap finding without making a command/merger claim
the sources do not support.
