# 764 — Network Profile

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-19
**Confidence:** E for designation/legal documents; C for independent research;
A for synthesis.

---

## 1. Status

**764 is a designated terrorist entity in Canada** (listed 2025-12-10; regulation
published 2025-12-17). New Zealand designated **O9A** in 2025; 764 is described as
part of the same extremist ecosystem. The United States has prosecuted 764-linked
cases, but this review did not find a US designation of 764.

**764 is not a single company or a single leader.** It is described by governments
and researchers as a decentralised transnational network of nihilistic violent
extremists, with cells, status hierarchies, and fluid membership across the Com
and adjacent networks.

---

## 2. Victim and recruitment pattern

Primary/independent sources describe:

- targeting minors and vulnerable youth;
- entry through gaming, Discord, Telegram, and youth-heavy online spaces;
- grooming and coercion, including sextortion and forced/encouraged self-harm;
- animal cruelty and other degradation as status/entry tests;
- recording and circulation of abuse material within the network;
- manuals/instructional material;
- pressure toward offline violence in some cases.

This profile deliberately omits graphic detail. The point is the **pattern**, not
the content.

## 4. Legal and research record

- Canada: Public Safety listing and Canada Gazette regulation (2025).
- New Zealand: O9A designation (2025).
- US: FBI/DOJ cases and congressional interest; UNO NCTE report on prosecutions.
- Research: GNET, CTC West Point, GWU Program on Extremism, ADL, ISD, CNA.
- Independent analysis: 764/Com hybridisation and “remotely coerced violence.”

---

## 5. Why it matters to UTTP analysis

764 is the **reference ecosystem** for the overlap claim:

- both are decentralised and brand/cell-based;
- both target youth-heavy online spaces;
- both use grooming/recruitment and status-through-harm;
- membership is reported as fluid across groups;
- both migrate across platforms to survive enforcement.

That is **tactic/ecosystem overlap**. It is not proof that UTTP is 764 or that
UTTP centrally coordinates 764.

---

## 6. Limits

- 764 is not monolithic; cells differ.
- Not every UTTP faction is 764-linked.
- Public reporting is shaped by platform removals and victim privacy.
- Do not repeat graphic details or private victim information.
