## Headline finding

The evidence supports **edge fusion: substantive overlap in tactic families,
membership, and joint activity** between UTTP and the 764 ecosystem. It does
**not** support saying “UTTP is an operational arm of 764,” “UTTP merged with
764,” “UTTP is 764,” “UTTP is a designated terrorist entity,” or “764 centrally
commands UTTP.”

See [`FINAL_ASSESSMENT_2026-11-19.md`](FINAL_ASSESSMENT_2026-11-19.md) for the
claim-by-claim test.

- **764** is a designated terrorist entity in Canada (2025); New Zealand
  designated O9A/ONA (2025-12-07), and the NZ O9A statement of case lists 764 as
  an O9A **nexion**. Nexions are operationally independent; see
  [`14_O9A_764_TRACE/`](../14_O9A_764_TRACE/).
- **UTTP** is a decentralised harassment/NVE-adjacent brand. Independent analysis
  (Sparx Intelligence, 2026-07-15) explicitly describes overlap with 764/NLM/MMC,
  grooming “similarly to 764,” and fluid membership across groups.
- WIC’s archive documents a UTTP–764 recruitment/collaboration pipeline; those
  specific internal claims are **A/S** (WIC/community) unless a primary source is
  attached.
- Bx Writes (2026-06-25) describes UTTP/Foodist groups as having “recently begun
  to fuse with parts of extortion Com, like the 764 network.” **C/A**
- Soyjak Wiki (2026-11-18) says UTTP became “intertwined” with 764/Com around
  2023–25. **D**

## Why this folder exists

The overlap is not a slogan. It is a set of testable claims. This folder makes
each claim testable: tactic, source, confidence, what it proves, and what it does
not.
