## What this archive is

A structured record of **UTTP (UTube Troll Police)**, its factions, offshoots,
parent entities, affiliates, defenders, and the ecosystems it has targeted. It
combines WIC OSINT, donated documentation, public-source research, screenshots,
exports, and historical records.

The archive is intentionally conservative about claims. Read
[CLAIM_LEDGER.md](CLAIM_LEDGER.md) before citing a specific allegation. The
ledger separates:

- **Established** — government, court, law-enforcement, or primary documents
- **Corroborated** — two or more independent sources
- **Assessed** — WIC analysis from the available record
- **Alleged / single-source** — not independently established
- **Community documentation** — wikis, directories, and donated notes
- **Disputed / superseded** — old claims that should not be repeated as fact
