# Area Intelligence — Methodology

**Prepared:** 2026-11-19

## 1. What an “area” is

An area is a **platform surface** (for example, YouTube comments, Discord
servers, X replies, Telegram channels), not a person, account, or invite link.

The register answers: *where has UTTP activity been reported or assessed?*

It does **not** answer: *who is UTTP?* or *give me accounts to contact.*

## 2. Status labels

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Observed | Direct archive capture or platform observation. |
| Reported | Independent/community reporting or WIC analysis. |
| Assessed | Analytic inference from ecosystem behavior. |
| Historical | Former area; not necessarily current. |

## 3. Confidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| C | Corroborated by two or more independent sources. |
| A | WIC assessment. |
| D | Community/raw documentation. |
| X | Disputed/superseded. |

## 4. Counting rule

A row is a **platform/surface area**, not a confirmed account or a unique user.
Two rows for the same platform are different surfaces, not two people.

**200 rows ≠ 200 accounts.**

## 5. Inclusion rule

Included:

- platforms/surfaces with archive, reporting, or analytic support for UTTP or
  UTTP-adjacent activity;
- historical areas relevant to UTTP’s lineage;
- public archive/wiki/forum layers used for documentation or cross-posting.

Excluded:

- private groups, invite links, account IDs, personal handles, victim data;
- unverified StyxNet profile matches (see `09_STYXNET_REPORTS/validation/`);
- speculative platforms with no support in the record.

## 6. Limits

- Presence is not a census.
- A platform surface may host many unrelated users.
- “Assessed” rows are leads, not confirmed observations.
- Historical platforms may have no current UTTP presence.
- Do not convert this register into a target list.

## 7. Ethical use

This register is for defenders, platforms, researchers, and lawful authorities.
It does not authorise contact, infiltration, harassment, or retaliation.
