# UTTP Area Intelligence — 2026-11-19

**Classification:** public OSINT / group-level

**Scope:** digital areas where UTTP activity has been reported or assessed. This is not an account list and contains no usernames, account IDs, invite links, victim data, or private identifiers.

> **Not a target list.** Areas are platforms/surfaces, not people. Presence is not a census and not proof that every UTTP faction uses every area.

## How to read this

- **Observed** — direct archive capture or platform observation.
- **Reported** — independent/community reporting or WIC analysis.
- **Assessed** — analytic inference from the ecosystem record.
- **Historical** — former area, not necessarily current.

- Confidence: **C** corroborated, **A** assessed, **D** community/raw, **X** disputed.
