## Rule

A source supports only what it actually says. A platform label in the archive is
not proof that UTTP owns or controls that platform area. A StyxNet match is not a
member. A community wiki is not a court finding.

## What was not used

- Individual account IDs, private handles, invite links, victim data.
- Unverified StyxNet profile URLs as “UTTP accounts.”
- Fabricated naming-pattern entries as members.
