## Important distinction

The raw `General List.txt` mixes three different things:

1. **Known public handles** — leads, not ownership-confirmed identities.
2. **Naming conventions** — templates such as `OfficerAmerica` or `DPOS_Command`.
   These are patterns, not confirmed accounts.
3. **Group names** — UTTP, DPOS, 764, FPE-Police, AUTTP, etc.

The expanded register separates these categories. Do not treat a naming pattern
as a member.

## Confidence

- **E** — primary document or direct observation.
- **C** — two or more independent sources.
- **A** — WIC assessment.
- **S** — single-source/unverified.
- **D** — community/raw input.
- **X** — disputed/superseded.
