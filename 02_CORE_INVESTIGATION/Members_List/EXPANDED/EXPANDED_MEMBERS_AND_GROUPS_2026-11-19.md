## 5. Why the listings matter

- **UTTP** is a brand; these listings show how the brand splits into factions.
- **DPOS** is the structured offshoot: its units show how harassment is
  organised under a police-state aesthetic.
- **764/O9A/Spawnism** are adjacent ecosystems, not UTTP subsidiaries.
- **GateKeeper** is a defence tool, not an ally.
- **Naming patterns** are useful for detection but dangerous if mistaken for
  members.
