## 1. Bottom line

UTTP (UTube Troll Police / YouTube Troll Police) is a **decentralised trolling
and harassment network/brand** that emerged in 2011. By 2026 it is fragmented:
legacy and successor factions, splinters, offshoots, and self-described
“defenders” operate under or around the UTTP name. It is not a single company,
membership list, or command hierarchy.

The most consequential current finding is not “UTTP is one group.” It is that
UTTP-style ecosystems **overlap with nihilistic violent extremist (NVE) networks**
and that the wider NVE ecosystem — especially **764** and **O9A** — has been
designated, prosecuted, and studied by governments and independent researchers.

**UTTP itself is not a designated terrorist entity in the sources reviewed.**
Canada designated **764**; New Zealand designated **O9A/ONA** on 2025-12-07. Independent analysis
(Sparx Intelligence, July 2026) describes UTTP as an NVE network with overlap to
764, NLM, and MMC. That is an analytic assessment, not a legal designation.

## 2. What is established, assessed, and alleged

### Established (E)

- **Canada listed 764 as a terrorist entity** on 2025-12-10. The regulation was
  published in the Canada Gazette on 2025-12-17.
- **New Zealand designated O9A/ONA** on 2025-12-07; its statement of case lists
  764 as an O9A **nexion**. Nexions are operationally independent, so this is a
  network position, not a command relationship.
- **US DOJ/FBI and Canadian RCMP cases** document serious 764-linked child
  exploitation, coercion, and cyberstalking.
- **Independent academic/think-tank work** (GNET, CTC West Point, GWU Program on
  Extremism, UNO NCTE) treats 764/O9A-adjacent NVE as a real, cross-platform
  threat to minors.

### Corroborated / independently analysed (C)

- **UTTP began in 2011** as a YouTube/Google+ trolling group and evolved toward
  raids, harassment, account takeover, and denial-of-service activity.
- **UTTP is decentralised** and has multiple factions/splinters; the “brand”
  persists even when a faction is disrupted.
- **UTTP targets fandom and creator communities**, including youth-heavy spaces.
- **Independent analysis** describes UTTP as an NVE network with overlap to
  other nihilistic extremist networks.

### WIC assessment (A)

- UTTP’s later factions function as a **recruitment/overlap pipeline** into the
  764/O9A-adjacent ecosystem.
- UTTP’s “brand overcoat” model lets activity continue after a takedown.
- WIC’s FPE engagement was a **controlled intelligence operation**, not an FPE
  partnership or governance role.

### Alleged / community documentation (S/D)

- Specific claims about which faction recruited which people, who attended which
  server, who wrote which message, and who “controls” UTTP now.
- “UTTP is in retreat” / “complete DNB” / “almost no members” — current community
  wiki assessments, not measured census data.
- “FBI Tier 1” — repeated in the archive, but a primary FBI document using that
  label was not located in this review. Do not cite it as established.

## 5. Why the ecosystem persists

1. **Decentralisation.** No single server or leader is a single point of failure.
2. **Platform gaps.** Automated enforcement can be gamed; appeals are slow.
3. **Youth-heavy target spaces.** Fandoms and creator communities are exposed to
   grooming, shock content, and reputation attacks.
4. **Brand reuse.** A takedown removes an account, not the name or playbook.
5. **Cheap anonymity.** VPNs, alt accounts, and cross-platform migration lower
   the cost of returning.
6. **Documentation gaps.** Victims and platforms often lack structured,
   preserved evidence; the harm becomes unprovable.
