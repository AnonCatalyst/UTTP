# Open Questions

These are research gaps, not accusations.

## Structure and continuity

- How many active UTTP-branded spaces exist in 2026, and how are they connected?
- Which factions are ideological, financial, or attention-driven?
- How much of the “UTTP” brand is coordinated versus imitated?
- What happens to a faction after a server nuke or platform ban?

## Recruitment and harm

- What pathways bring minors into UTTP-adjacent spaces?
- How do platforms’ recommendation and DM systems contribute?
- What is the overlap between UTTP-style harassment and 764/NVE recruitment in
  practice, not just in rhetoric?
- What victim-support and exit pathways exist?

## Platform enforcement

- Which platform policies are actually applied to UTTP-style mass reporting and
  ban evasion?
- How quickly do platforms preserve evidence after a report?
- What is the appeal/reinstatement pattern for removed accounts?

## Infrastructure

- Which hosting, CDN, and payment providers recur across UTTP-adjacent spaces?
- How do actors rotate domains, accounts, and invite links?
- What can defenders block without harming bystanders?

## Legal and accountability

- Which cases have produced charges, convictions, or designations?
- Where are the jurisdictional gaps?
- What evidence standards do platforms and authorities actually require?

## Measurement

- Can “activity” be measured without exposing private users?
- What would a reproducible, ethical UTTP activity index look like?
- How do we separate bot noise, research scanners, and hostile actors?

## Archive quality

- Which old claims need a primary source or a correction?
- Which files are duplicates or multipart sets?
- Which documents are WIC analysis versus independent evidence?
- What should be added next without expanding the private-data surface?

See `VERIFICATION_QUEUE.md` for the actionable list.
