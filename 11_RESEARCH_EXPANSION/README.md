## Standards

1. **No new private data.** Group names and public-source links only.
2. **No target list.** This is not an instruction to contact or act against anyone.
3. **Label confidence.** Use E/C/A/S/D/X as defined in `00_READ_FIRST/CLAIM_LEDGER.md`.
4. **Do not treat WIC analysis as independent evidence.** WIC wrote it; say so.
5. **Do not treat a community wiki as a court finding.** It is a lead, not a verdict.
6. **Preserve the original.** Corrections go in the ledger; raw evidence is not rewritten.

## Headline assessment

UTTP is best understood in 2026 as a **decentralised harassment brand and
splinter ecosystem**, not a single organisation with one command chain. It began
as a YouTube/Google+ trolling group in 2011, evolved through multiple leadership
and faction eras, and later developed overlaps with nihilistic violent extremist
(NVE) networks, most notably the 764/O9A-adjacent ecosystem.

The strongest independent findings are about the **wider NVE ecosystem**:
Canada listed 764 as a terrorist entity in December 2025; New Zealand designated
O9A; FBI/DOJ cases document serious 764-linked exploitation. The UTTP-specific
link is an **assessment** supported by WIC records, community documentation, and
independent analysis — not a designated-entity finding against UTTP itself.
