## Verification standard

A claim is promoted only when:

1. the primary source is attached or linked;
2. the source actually says the claim (not a paraphrase of a paraphrase);
3. the date and context are recorded;
4. a second independent source exists for a disputed fact;
5. the claim ledger is updated with the new confidence label.

## Never publish

- home addresses, employers, family details, phone numbers, private emails;
- account credentials, session tokens, or private messages obtained without consent;
- instructions to contact, join, or act against a person or private space.
