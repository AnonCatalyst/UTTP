# INBOX — post new UTTP tracking here

This is the drop folder for new **group-level** observations about UTTP: what it
is doing, what it is planning publicly, which methods it is using, and what has
changed.

**Do not post private data, doxxes, credentials, or material from private
spaces.** Do not post an instruction to contact or act against anyone. If the
material is sensitive, report it to the authorities first and contact WIC
privately.

## How to submit

1. Copy `_TEMPLATE.md` to a new file in this folder.
2. Name it `YYYY-MM-DD_short_slug.md` (example: `2026-11-19_new-forum.md`).
3. Fill in the source URL(s), UTC time, platform, group/faction, observation,
   method, and confidence.
4. Open a pull request or commit it with a short message.
5. A maintainer will triage it into the tracking board/register or ask for
   corroboration.

## What belongs here

- new UTTP-branded servers, forums, domains, or campaigns
- public changes in faction status or claims
- new/adapted methods observed in public
- public evidence of platform migration, mass reporting, raids, impersonation,
  or ban evasion
- corrections to existing archive claims

## What does not belong here

- private individuals or personal identifiers
- private messages or leaked personal data
- calls to harass, infiltrate, or “expose” anyone
- unverified claims presented as fact
- graphic media; link to a public source and describe the category instead

## Confidence

Use `E/C/A/S/D/X` from `00_READ_FIRST/CLAIM_LEDGER.md`. If you are unsure, use
`S` (single-source) or `D` (community documentation) and say why.
