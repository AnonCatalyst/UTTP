# INBOX triage

1. **Privacy check** — reject anything with private identifiers, credentials,
   private messages, or a call to action.
2. **Source check** — is the URL public, dated, and accessible?
3. **Confidence** — assign E/C/A/S/D/X.
4. **Corroboration** — look for a second independent source.
5. **Route** — update `TRACKING_BOARD.md`, `TRACKING_REGISTER.csv`,
   `METHODS/METHOD_TRACKER.csv`, and/or `CLAIM_LEDGER.md`.
6. **Preserve** — keep the original note in Git history; do not silently rewrite
   a contributor's observation.
7. **Escalate** — imminent harm/exploitation goes to the authorities; do not
   investigate further.

Contributors are not asked to infiltrate, entrap, or engage anyone.
