# UTTP Tracking Cell — group-level intelligence

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-19
**Scope:** track UTTP as a **brand, faction ecosystem, and set of public
behaviours**. Not a list of people. Not a target list. Not an infiltration plan.

- what changed,
- what is verified vs community-reported,
- what to watch next,
- what not to do.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Group-level only.** No private individuals, addresses, employers, family,
   phone numbers, private emails, or account credentials.
2. **No engagement.** Do not join, message, infiltrate, bait, or provoke anyone.
   The archive is documentation, not an operation.
3. **No target list.** The register exists to understand a network, not to direct
   action against anyone.
4. **Label confidence.** Use E/C/A/S/D/X from `00_READ_FIRST/CLAIM_LEDGER.md`.
5. **Preserve the source.** Link it, date it, quote only what is necessary.
6. **Escalate harm, not gossip.** Credible threats of violence or exploitation
   go to the authorities, not to a public thread.

## Current headline

UTTP is best tracked as a **persistent brand + fragmented faction ecosystem**.
The brand survives takedowns; individual factions do not. As of late 2026,
community documentation describes UTTP as heavily reduced (“complete DNB”/
almost no members) while splinters and adjacent NVE networks continue. That is a
community assessment, not a census, and it does not mean the brand is gone.

UTTP itself is **not** a designated terrorist entity in the sources reviewed.
**764** (Canada, 2025) and **O9A/ONA** (New Zealand, 2025-12-07) are designated; NZ lists 764 as an O9A nexion, but nexions are operationally independent. UTTP's
relationship to that ecosystem is an **assessment**, with independent analysis
describing edge fusion/overlap; no merger or operational-arm relationship is
established.
