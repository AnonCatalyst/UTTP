# Collection Plan — public-source, ethical tracking

**Goal:** track UTTP as a group/brand ecosystem over time without engaging,
infiltrating, or targeting people.

## 1. Sources

### Primary/public record

- Government designations, court records, law-enforcement releases.
- Platform transparency reports and public enforcement notices.
- Public academic/think-tank research.
- Public posts from UTTP-branded group accounts (observe only; do not reply).
- Public wikis/directories (Soyjak Wiki, directories) as **leads**.
- This archive and its donated material.

### Do not use

- Private servers, DMs, paid leaks, hacked data, or purchased personal data.
- Anything requiring an account to access a private space.
- Any form of infiltration, baiting, or provocation of individuals.
- Scraping logged-in users or private profiles.

## 2. Cadence

| Cadence | Task | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | Check source URLs; scan for new public UTTP-branded spaces/campaign names | `TRACKING_BOARD.md` update |
| Monthly | Rebuild group-level register; verify dates/links; update confidence | `TRACKING_REGISTER.csv` + snapshot note |
| Quarterly | Source-quality review; remove broken links; reconcile old claims | `CLAIM_LEDGER.md` update |
| On event | New designation, arrest, server nuke, campaign, or archive donation | incident note + source ledger |

Do not use these queries to find or contact private individuals.

## 4. Recording standard

Every entry needs:

- group-level name (no private person),
- date/time observed (UTC),
- URL/source,
- confidence E/C/A/S/D/X,
- what changed,
- whether it is copied from a community source or independently verified.

## 5. Metrics

Track **coverage and change**, not people:

- number of active public group-level sources,
- number of new group/brand/faction names,
- source freshness (last checked),
- corroboration rate,
- open verification items,
- archive additions.

## 6. Escalation

If a public source indicates imminent violence, child exploitation, or a credible
threat to a identifiable person, do not investigate further. Preserve the public
link, and report to the appropriate authority (NCMEC, FBI IC3, local law
enforcement). Do not publish private data.

## 7. Review

This plan is public. Any change to scope must preserve the non-engagement and
no-private-data rules.
